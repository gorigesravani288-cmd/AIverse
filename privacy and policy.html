🔒 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Email address and name (when you sign in with Google)
  • Usage Data: Tools you view, save, rate, and collections you create
  • Submitted Tools: Tools you suggest through the submit form

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Personalize your AI tool recommendations
  • Improve our platform and tool suggestions
  • Send notifications about new tools (optional)
  • Process tool submissions and community collections

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on Supabase (PostgreSQL database) with encrypted connections.

We use Google OAuth - we never store your password.

4. AI & Data Usage

When you use AI features, your request is sent to Google Gemini API.

Only the text of your request is sent (not personal info).

5. Your Rights

  • Access: Request a copy of your data
  • Delete: Request deletion of your account and data
  • Opt-out: Turn off AI features in Settings

6. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase: Authentication and database
  • Google: OAuth login and Gemini API

7. Cookies

We use essential cookies for session management. No tracking cookies.

8. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We'll notify users of material changes. Continued use constitutes acceptance.
